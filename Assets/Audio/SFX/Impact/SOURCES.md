# Impact SFX Sources

All final clips in this folder were edited, layered, filtered, and normalized for
Three Doors of Fate from CC0 source material.

- Kenney Impact Sounds: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- OpenGameArt Stone Door: https://opengameart.org/content/stone-door
- OpenGameArt Metal Impact Sounds: https://opengameart.org/content/metal-impact-sounds
- OpenGameArt Swishes Sound Pack: https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
- OpenGameArt 3 Dark Magic Spells: https://opengameart.org/content/3-dark-magic-spells
- OpenGameArt 100 CC0 SFX #2: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2

Final clips are rendered as mono 48 kHz PCM WAV files with headroom preserved for
in-game layering.
