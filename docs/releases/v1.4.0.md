## Player-facing changes

### A run now begins with a deliberate identity

- Gambler, Oracle, and Exile each use a deterministic 24-card starter deck rather than a random 30-card start.
- Every class offers three contracts, nine total. A contract can swap up to four cards and make bounded changes to starting HP, Gold, Luck, or Debt.
- Contract details show the card and resource changes before confirmation.

### Card choices require an explicit commitment

- Combat, reward, shop, treasure, and deck-removal cards use one shared top-layer inspection panel.
- Inspecting a card never plays, buys, takes, or removes it. The player must press the context-specific confirmation action.
- Rest sites and shops can remove cards while protecting a minimum 12-card deck and at least four Attack cards. Repeated shop removals become more expensive during the run.

### Runs resume consistently on every difficulty

- Checkpoint v2 covers Easy, Normal, Hard, and Endless at stable transition points.
- Run seed, random cursor, contract, removals, seen events, mutations, door offers, encounters, shops, and rewards are persisted.
- Closing during combat restores that encounter from its beginning without rerolling it.
- Existing version 1 checkpoints migrate through the compatibility reader; failed restoration does not erase unrelated local or iCloud progression.

### Encounters and long runs ask for different decisions

- Eight data-driven fate events include four universal, three class-specific, and one high-Debt event.
- Twenty standard enemies and four bosses have explicit behavior bindings, unique actions, selection guards, and visible boss phases.
- Endless offers one of three non-duplicate Abyss mutations every five doors. Six mutations pair a risk with a reward instead of adding only raw enemy scaling.

### Fate History makes completed runs useful

- The latest ten completed runs are stored locally with class, contract, difficulty, furthest door, outcome, rewards, and one of seven comic epithets.
- Main-menu and detail views expose the history without adding Game Center identifiers or points.
- Game-over history details sit below the existing central ending copy so the ending remains readable.

## Compatibility and retained policies

- Existing collectibles, unlocks, local saves, iCloud progression, and permanent Game Center IDs are preserved.
- Game Center remains at 20 achievements and 1,000 total points; v1.4.0 adds no achievements.
- Optional rewarded relic ads remain the only ads. There are no forced, interstitial, or banner ads, IAP, or subscriptions.
- Existing menu, exploration, combat, and boss background music remains; removed combat and UI sound effects are not restored.

## Intentionally deferred

- No fourth class, new region, PvP, server analytics, Android account sync, additional language, or full 72-card rebalance.
- No iOS export, upload, App Store Connect mutation, tag, or public release occurs from this release-candidate preparation step.
