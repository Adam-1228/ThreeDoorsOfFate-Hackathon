# Three Doors of Fate v1.3.0

This release focuses on five high-priority fairness, clarity, and achievement changes.

- Boss attack intent and resolved damage now use one final-damage calculation.
- Eligible Attack guarantees cover two- to four-card offers.
- Treasure cards can be taken or skipped after automatic Gold collection.
- Easy and Normal boss fights receive one conservative no-Attack full-hand correction per combat.
- The unreleased 50-card achievement is replaced by a hidden achievement for three identical explicit rerolls in one battle, with new dedicated artwork.
