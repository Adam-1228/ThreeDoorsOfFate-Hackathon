# Notices

## AI-assisted production

Three Doors of Fate was created through human-directed, AI-assisted production. AI tools supported game design, programming, visual and music generation, testing, iteration, and documentation. The project owner selected, edited, integrated, and verified the released results.

The project owner confirms that the original runtime art under `Assets/Art` and the background music under `Assets/Audio/Music` were created by the owner with AI assistance and are authorized for public distribution in this repository and the released game build. They remain all rights reserved unless explicitly stated otherwise.
